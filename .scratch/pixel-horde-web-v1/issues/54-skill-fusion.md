# 54: Skill Fusion (design first)

**What to build:** Owner idea (2026-09-27): once general skills are evolved, two of them (never Awakened skills) can be
**fused** for Gold. Both disappear and a new fused skill appears with a new form. It starts again at level 1, but its
power builds on the two base skills: "an Awakening for normal skills".

Open design questions (grill the owner before any code):
- Recipes: a fixed list (recommended: 6–8 recipes, e.g. by element pairs) or any pair of the 12 (66 pairs, too many)?
- Can the Signature be fused? (Suggest no: its slot is locked and it has its own Awakening.)
- Where and when: clear screen only, cost in Gold × Chapter? One fusion per Stage end?
- What "power from the base skills" means in numbers: fused level 1 = both base skills' max-level damage × k?
- Does fusing a Link break the Awakening requirements (Links must stay maxed and equipped)?
- Fused skills and Evolution / Combos / the Shadow Clone / co-op.

Deliverable of the design step: a short spec + the recipe table, then implementation tickets.
